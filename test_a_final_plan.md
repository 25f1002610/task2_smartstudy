## 💡 High-Yield Exam Day Tips
1. **Time Strategy:** Spend the first 10% of your exam time reading all problems. Allocate time proportionally based on point values; do not get stuck on a single graph or DP problem for more than 40% of your allotted time.
2. **Edge-Case Checklist:** Always check for empty inputs (`null` or length 0), single-node trees/lists, cycles in pointers/graphs, negative weight edges in graphs, and array bounds/off-by-one errors in loops.
3. **Calm Execution:** If you blank out on an advanced algorithm (like Dijkstra or DP state transitions), write out the brute-force or recursive approach first, then optimize or memoize step-by-step to secure partial credit.
